# G8-T4, ρ = 10: DRAM-only / SRAM-only / DRAM + SRAM results, six models

Generated by `tools/g8_cache/build_t4_results.py --rho 10` from the VERIFIED campaign runs; do not edit by hand (re-run the script). The same numbers are in [G8_T4_RHO10_RESULTS.csv](G8_T4_RHO10_RESULTS.csv), with each row's run directory. The ρ = 1 results are in [G8_T4_RESULTS.md](G8_T4_RESULTS.md).

- **Modes.** DRAM-only = the G7-v2 runs (`artifacts/g7/campaign`), the same runs as in the ρ = 1 report; SRAM-only = L2 cache faults only (`artifacts/g8/rho10/sram_only/campaign`); DRAM + SRAM = both (`artifacts/g8/rho10/campaign`).
- **Levels.** The seven DRAM BERs 1e-7 … 1e-5. Cache BER = 10 × the level's DRAM BER = 1e-6 … 1e-4 (frozen ρ = 10, [G8_T3_SRAM_BER_DERIVATION.md](G8_T3_SRAM_BER_DERIVATION.md)). ResNet-50 runs a nine-level ladder; its L3–L9 are these seven BERs.
- **Trials.** 100 per level and mode, 10,000 images each. Completed trials (including DUE) + crash trials = 100.
- **Top-1** is the mean over normally completed trials: DUE (first invalid output aborts the trial) and crash (PROCESS_FATAL, the runner process died) trials are excluded and counted separately, as in G7-v2. The mean is over 100 − DUE − crash trials (`trials_averaged` in the CSV). At ρ = 10 that count gets small: EfficientNet-B0 and Swin-T crash in 69–85 trials at 7e-6 and 1e-5 in both cache modes, so those cells average 14–22 trials, most of them collapsed.
- **Bits per trial.** DRAM bits are the frozen B of the level (DRAM-only and DRAM + SRAM). SRAM bits are n_cache = round(10 × BER × R_eff_bits), measured per process on an idle GPU 0 (SRAM-only and DRAM + SRAM). They are ≈ 10 × the DRAM bits except for ViT-B (≈ 7.9 ×), which is larger than L2 (R_eff ≈ 79 % of R).
- **Injection surface.** Every model injects over its full frozen R in all three modes except MobileNetV3-L, whose R is exclusion-scoped: its `trt-internal-5/6` ctx-phase pools (248,320 B, 2.66 % of the measured 9,336,232 B) are process-fatal on any hit and are excluded from the DRAM faults, the cache faults and the residency probe ([G7V2_RESULTS.md](G7V2_RESULTS.md) §4, user decision 2026-09-27).
- **Checks.** Every accuracy matches the points CSV of the model's figure (`artifacts/g8/rho10/fig/<model>_threeway.png`) to 0.005 pp. Every cache-mode run passes the idle-GPU audit (`tools/g8_cache/audit_idle.py --rho 10`, G8 plan §15.5).
- **DRAM-only.** These columns are identical to the ρ = 1 report and match `G7V2_RESULTS.md` §3 (see the ρ = 1 report for the Swin-T L4 / L6 DUE correction of 2026-10-08).
